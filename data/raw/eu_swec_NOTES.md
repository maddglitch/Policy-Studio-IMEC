# EU Single Window Environment for Customs (EU SWE-C) — documentary notes

Added July 2026. Documentary (legal-text) evidence, no CSV — this layer is a
legislated timeline, not a survey.

## What it is
**Regulation (EU) 2022/2399** (European Parliament and Council, 23 Nov 2022)
establishes the EU Single Window Environment for Customs. It is the EU-side
evidence for the paper's third step ("connect to the EU's mature system").

## Timeline (from the Regulation and DG TAXUD)
- **12 Dec 2022** — Regulation in force.
- **2025** — G2G layer mandatory: **EU CSW-CERTEX** connects national customs
  systems to EU regulatory systems for automatic exchange/verification of
  non-customs formalities (initially SPS, organics, some environmental,
  cultural goods).
- **2026–2028** — CERTEX domain expansion (deforestation, food/feed, animal
  and plant health, dual-use, ozone, F-gases, timber, CITES…).
- **13 Dec 2031** — B2G layer applies: single submission point for economic
  operators (remaining provisions of the Regulation).

## How the paper uses it
1. The "EU mature system" claim becomes **documentary rather than inferred**
   (it previously leaned on the EU TFA entry-into-force inference alone).
2. **Even the EU's internal cross-agency single window completes only in
   2031** — independent support for the core thesis that the cross-border /
   cross-agency layer is the hard part, not the national technical build.
3. Gives the UAE–EU CEPA negotiation a concrete EU-side landing surface: a
   corridor single window that wants to interoperate with the EU has a dated,
   funded system (and standards) to target.
